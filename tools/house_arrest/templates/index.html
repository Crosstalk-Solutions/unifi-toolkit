What no firewall rule here can stop

can still see and profile everything sharing — discovery traffic between neighbours never passes the gateway, so no policy on this page ever sees it. A smart TV doing this is what the recent LG reporting describes.

Two things actually stop it:

  • Give it a dedicated VLAN of its own, in UniFi itself — assign the device's switch port (wired) or a dedicated Wi-Fi network (wireless) to a VLAN nothing else lives on. That doesn't filter peer traffic; it removes the peers. Then lock that VLAN down from the Networks and DNS tabs here. House Arrest deliberately doesn't move devices between VLANs itself — a per-client move can leave a device half-connected, which is worse than either state.
  • Turn on Client Isolation for its Wi-Fi network, on the Networks tab. Wireless only, and it applies to every device on that SSID.
